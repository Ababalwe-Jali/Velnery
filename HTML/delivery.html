Delivery questions

What if I'm not home?

The courier will try twice. Add instructions at checkout, like leaving your order with security or a neighbour.

Can I change my delivery address after ordering?

Contact us within an hour of placing your order and we'll do our best to update it before it is packed.

Do you deliver on public holidays?

No. Deliveries happen on working days only, so a public holiday adds one day to your delivery time.
