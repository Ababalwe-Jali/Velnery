Who we are

VELNERY (“we”, “us”) is an online store based in South Africa. We are the “responsible party” for personal information collected through this website under the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

What information we collect

We only collect what we need to run the store and look after you.

  • Account details: your name, email address and a password (stored only as a one-way hash).
  • Order details: items bought, delivery address, phone number and order history.
  • Payment details: the payment method you choose. We never store your full card number or CVV.
  • Returns and support: the information you give us when you request a return or contact us.
  • Preferences: your cart, wishlist, saved addresses and cookie choices.

How we use your information

  • To process and deliver your orders and handle returns and refunds.
  • To create and manage your account.
  • To answer your questions and give customer support.
  • To keep the website safe, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations.
  • To send you marketing, only if you have agreed. You can opt out at any time.

We process your information because it is needed to carry out your order, because the law requires it, because we have a legitimate interest in running a safe store, or because you have given consent.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with people who help us serve you:

  • Couriers and delivery partners, who need your name, address and phone number.
  • Payment providers, who process your payment securely.
  • Service providers such as hosting and email, who act on our instructions.
  • Authorities, when the law requires us to.

How this demo stores data

In this portfolio version there is no server. Your cart, wishlist, addresses, account, orders and return requests are saved in your own browser (local storage) and never leave your device. Clearing your browser data removes them. See our Cookie policy for the full list.

How long we keep it and how we protect it

We keep order records for as long as the law requires (usually five years for tax and accounting) and account details until you ask us to delete them. We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections and limited access.

Your rights

Under POPIA you may:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct or delete it;
  • object to us processing it, or withdraw your consent;
  • object to direct marketing at any time; and
  • complain to the Information Regulator at inforegulator.org.za if you think we have handled your information unlawfully.

To use any of these rights, email velnery@gmail.com.

Children

Our store is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18 without a parent or guardian’s consent.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of the page shows when it last changed. If we make a significant change we will let you know on the website.
