What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files a website stores on your device. Websites can also use similar technology such as local storage, which keeps information in your browser. We refer to all of these as “cookies” on this page.

How we use them

VELNERY uses browser storage mainly to make the store work: to keep your cart, wishlist and sign-in. Right now the site does not use analytics or advertising cookies, and it does not set third-party tracking cookies. If we add them later we will ask for your consent first.

  • Essential: needed for the store to work. These cannot be switched off.
  • Analytics: would help us understand how the site is used. Optional.
  • Marketing: would show you relevant offers. Optional.

Manage your choices

Control cookies in your browser

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Be aware that if you clear browser storage your cart, wishlist, addresses and account on this device will be removed, and parts of the store may stop working.
