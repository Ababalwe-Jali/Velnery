About VELNERY

VELNERY is a South African online store for fashion, home and living, beauty and food, with simple pricing, fast delivery and easy returns.

Our aim is to make everyday shopping feel considered and effortless. We pair a clean, uncluttered shop with the service people expect from a trusted retailer: free delivery over R600, 30-day returns and flexible payment with Payflex.
