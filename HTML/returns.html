What you can return

  • Clothing that is unworn, unwashed and has its tags
  • Homeware in its original packaging
  • Unopened beauty and personal care items
  • Faulty or damaged items of any kind

What we can't take back

  • Perishable food and fresh produce
  • Opened beauty and personal care products
  • Gift cards
  • Items damaged after delivery
