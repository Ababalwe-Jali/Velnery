Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about orders, delivery, returns and payments.

How do I place an order?

Browse the shop, add what you like to your cart and go to checkout. You can check out as a guest or sign in first to save your details and track your orders.

Can I change or cancel my order?

If you contact us within an hour of placing your order we can usually change or cancel it. Once an order has been packed it can't be changed, but you can return it once it arrives.

How do I track my order?

Sign in and open My account, then Order history. You can also enter your order number (for example VEL-123456) on the My account page without signing in.

I didn't get an order confirmation email.

Check your spam or junk folder first, then make sure the email address on your order is correct. If it still hasn't arrived, contact us with your order number.

How much does delivery cost?

Standard delivery is R60, and free on orders over R600. Express delivery is R120. The delivery cost is shown at checkout before you pay.

How long will my order take?

Standard delivery takes 2 to 4 working days. Express delivery arrives the next working day for orders placed before 12:00. Remote areas can take a little longer.

Do you deliver on weekends?

We deliver on working days (Monday to Friday). Orders placed over a weekend are packed on the next working day.

What if I'm not home when my order arrives?

The courier will try to deliver twice. Add instructions at checkout, such as leaving it with security or a neighbour, and we'll pass them on.

What is your returns policy?

You can return most items within 30 days of delivery, as long as they are unused, unwashed and in their original packaging with tags attached.

How do I return an item?

Fill in the return request on our Returns page with your order number. We'll email you the next steps, and you can drop the parcel off or arrange a collection.

When will I receive my refund?

Refunds are paid within 5 to 7 working days after we receive and check your return. They go back to the original payment method.

Which items can't be returned?

For hygiene and safety, perishable food, opened beauty and personal care products, and gift cards can't be returned unless they arrive damaged or faulty.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept Visa and Mastercard cards, Payflex, Instant EFT and gift cards.

How does Payflex work?

Payflex splits your purchase into 4 interest-free payments. The first is taken at checkout and the rest follow every two weeks. Approval is subject to Payflex's checks.

Is my payment information safe?

Payments are encrypted, and we never store your full card number or CVV.

Do I need an account to order?

No. You can check out as a guest. An account lets you track orders, save addresses and keep a wishlist.

I forgot my password.

Choose Sign in, then Forgot password, and enter your email address. We'll send you a link to reset it.

How do I update my delivery address?

Use the Deliver to button at the top of any page, or open My account and go to Addresses to add, remove or choose your delivery address.
