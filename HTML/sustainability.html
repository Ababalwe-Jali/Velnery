Sustainability

How we are working to be a better store for people and the planet.

Doing better, one step at a time

We believe a good store should look after people and the planet. We set clear goals, share our progress and are honest about the work still to do.

Our focus areas

Responsible sourcing

We work with suppliers who treat workers fairly and care about materials. We favour South African makers and growers where we can.

  • Supplier code of conduct
  • Preference for local production
  • Traceable materials

Less packaging

We are cutting out unnecessary plastic and moving to recyclable and reusable packaging that still protects your order.

  • Recyclable boxes and paper fill
  • Right-sized parcels
  • No single-use plastic bags

Greener delivery

We group orders into fewer trips, plan efficient routes and work with couriers that are cutting their emissions.

  • Consolidated deliveries
  • Route planning
  • Click-and-collect points

Less waste

We donate unsold goods, repair or resell returns where possible and recycle what cannot be reused.

  • Donations of unsold stock
  • Returns checked for resale
  • Recycling partnerships

Our people

We aim to be a fair, safe and inclusive workplace with training and room to grow.

  • Fair pay
  • Safe working conditions
  • Learning and development

Community

We support local causes through food and clothing donations and by working with South African small businesses.

  • Food and clothing donations
  • Support for small suppliers
  • Volunteer days
